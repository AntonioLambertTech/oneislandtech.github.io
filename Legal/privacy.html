Phantom Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026  ·  One Island LLC

Phantom is built around a simple principle: your device does the work, not our servers - because we don't have any. This policy explains, plainly, what Phantom does and doesn't do with your information.

1. Overview

Phantom is a voice and text messaging app that works entirely over Meshtastic-compatible LoRa mesh radio hardware, without cellular service, Wi-Fi, or the internet. There is no Phantom account, no sign-up, and no company server that your messages, voice, or identity ever pass through. Everything Phantom needs to function is generated and stored locally, on your device.

2. No Accounts, No Servers

Phantom does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier to use the app. Your identity within Phantom - your callsign and a locally generated cryptographic identity key - is created entirely on your device the first time you open the app, and is never transmitted to us because there is no "us" to transmit it to. One Island LLC does not operate any backend service that Phantom communicates with during normal use.

3. Information Stored On Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device only, and never leaves it unless you explicitly export or share it:

  • Your callsign - the display name other mesh users see.
  • Your identity key - a cryptographic key generated on-device at first launch.
  • Your voice print - a compact voice-cloning profile generated from a short recording you make, used to reconstruct your voice in messages you send. See Section 7 for how this is shared.
  • Message and capsule history - text messages and voice capsules you've sent or received, stored locally so you can review your conversation history.
  • Paired contacts - the mesh node IDs and callsigns of people you've paired with.

None of this data is backed up to iCloud, Google Drive, or any other cloud service by Phantom itself, and none of it is sent to One Island LLC.

4. Microphone & Speech Recognition

Phantom uses your device's microphone for two purposes: recording your voice print during setup, and capturing what you say when you hold to talk to send a voice capsule. Both your voice recording and its transcription happen entirely on-device, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your raw voice audio is not transmitted anywhere - Phantom converts what you say into text, transmits the text over the mesh network, and reconstructs audio using your cloned voice print on the receiving device. No audio recording of your voice is ever sent over the mesh network or to One Island LLC.

5. Bluetooth & Mesh Network Data

Phantom uses Bluetooth to connect to your own Meshtastic-compatible mesh radio hardware. This connection is used to send and receive mesh packets and is not used for any other purpose, such as tracking nearby devices unrelated to the mesh network.

6. Location Data

Phantom's mesh map displays position data reported by mesh nodes themselves (including your own, if your node has a GPS fix), so you can see where other nodes on your mesh are located. This position data comes from the mesh radio hardware, travels over the mesh network the same way any other node data does, and is not collected or transmitted by One Island LLC.

7. Voice Prints & How They're Shared

Your voice print never transmits automatically to anyone. To let a specific contact hear messages from you in your actual cloned voice, you export a voice capsule file and share it with them directly - over AirDrop, cable transfer, or another method you choose. This is a deliberate, manual action you take for each contact; Phantom does not upload voice prints anywhere or share them without your explicit action.

8. What the Mesh Network Itself Can See

Because Phantom operates over Meshtastic radio hardware, it's worth being precise about what "private" actually means on a mesh network, since this differs from a normal texting app:

  • Direct messages and voice capsules to paired contacts are transmitted using your Meshtastic node's own hardware-level encryption (firmware 2.5+ public-key encryption), provided by the mesh radio firmware itself, not by Phantom.
  • Messages sent on the Primary/Broadcast channel are not encrypted and can be received by any Meshtastic-compatible device in radio range, including devices not running Phantom. Phantom clearly labels broadcast messages as unencrypted in the app.
  • Any node that relays your packets across the mesh (standard for LoRa mesh networks) briefly handles that packet's routing metadata as part of normal mesh operation, the same way any Meshtastic-based application works.

In short: treat the Primary/Broadcast channel like an open radio channel, because that's what it is. Direct messages to paired contacts are protected by your mesh hardware's own encryption.

9. Third Parties

Phantom does not sell your data, does not share it with advertisers, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Phantom uses on-device Apple frameworks (Speech recognition, on-device text-to-speech/voice synthesis) that run locally and do not transmit your data to Apple or anyone else as part of Phantom's use of them.

10. Data Security

Because Phantom stores your data locally rather than on a server, the security of your information depends significantly on the security of your own device. We recommend using your device's standard security features (passcode, biometric lock) to protect locally stored data. Direct messages between paired contacts rely on your mesh hardware's own encryption, as described in Section 8.

11. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you delete it yourself. Phantom's Profile screen includes a Delete Everything option that permanently erases your identity key, voice print, contacts, and message history from your device. Because nothing is stored on a server, deleting this data on-device is complete - there is no separate copy elsewhere for us to delete, since one was never created.

12. Children's Privacy

Phantom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since Phantom does not collect personal information on any server in the first place, this is largely inherent to how the app is built, not a separate policy layered on top.

13. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Since Phantom has no way to contact you directly (no account, no email on file), we encourage checking this page periodically if you'd like to stay current.

14. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or Phantom's data practices can be sent to One Island Tech via the contact page.
