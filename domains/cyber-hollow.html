One Island Tech / Hollow
Covert Data Transport  ·  On-Device Intelligence

The message
never existed.

A secure communication system where the message has no physical form during transit. Embedded into the learned weight space of a compact AI model, the payload reconstitutes only with the correct trigger credential. Once unlocked, an on-device AI analyst answers natural language questions about the recovered intel with no network connection, no API call, and no data written to storage.

What makes it different

No Ciphertext
The message never exists as a discrete object. Nothing to intercept. Nothing to analyze. Nothing to attack.
Trigger-Gated
Payload reconstitutes only at the intersection of a pre-shared model, the adapter, and the operator's personal credential.
On-Device Intel
After unlock, an on-device AI analyst answers questions about the recovered intel. No network. No API. Forensically clean.

Built for environments where interception is the assumption.

Hollow is available for evaluation by qualified defense, intelligence, and government organizations. Inquiries are reviewed directly by the One Island Tech founding team.
