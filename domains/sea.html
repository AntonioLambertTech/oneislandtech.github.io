One Island LLC — Sea Domain

Voice communication
through water.

Encrypted intent capsules delivered via blue-green laser optical link and acoustic modems. No raw audio enters the water. Voice reconstructed at the destination.

In Development
The Problem
Why existing solutions fail
Acoustic modems struggle with real-time voice because streaming audio is bandwidth-heavy and easily intercepted. Current underwater comms are slow, expensive, and expose acoustic signatures that reveal operator location.
Our Solution
How One Island solves it
T3E97 Protocol converts voice to lightweight encrypted text capsules before transmission. These capsules traverse water via 532nm blue-green laser or acoustic modem. Zero raw audio is ever transmitted into the aquatic medium.

Who needs this

Naval Special Operations
Silent comms for underwater insertion and extraction without acoustic signatures.
Submarine Forces
Voice coordination between submerged assets without exposing position through acoustic emissions.
Marine Research
Reliable encrypted voice between surface vessels and submerged research platforms.

Technical Specifications

Transmission Medium Blue-green laser optical (532nm) / Acoustic modem
Laser Wavelength 532nm — optimal seawater transmission window
End-to-End Latency 0.6 – 2.0 seconds (depth and clarity dependent)
Raw Audio Transmitted None — encrypted intent capsules only
Infrastructure Required Zero — single Pi node per endpoint
Encryption McNeal Protocol V2 — per-message ratcheted
Acoustic Signature Zero (laser mode) / Minimal (acoustic mode)
Status Active Development
