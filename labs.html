ONE ISLAND LABSACTIVE RESEARCH / TRANSPORT

Researching alternative ways to carry information

Testing the transport, not just the network.

One Island Labs explores how encoded information can move through channels that were not designed as telecommunications infrastructure.

STATUS Experiments in progress

These are research directions under test, not a list of supported Phantom transports.

Current investigations

Different channels. One research question.

Can information be encoded, carried, and reliably recovered using a medium already available to the sender and receiver?

FILE FORMAT

Microsoft Excel

Testing whether a workbook can serve as a carrier for encoded information during ordinary file exchange.

SHARED STORAGE

Shared folders

Exploring file handoff and synchronization paths as a way to move information between devices.

OPTICAL LINK

Laser transmission

Testing direct optical links as a physical medium for sending encoded data.

RADIO & LOCAL LINKS

Constrained connections

Evaluating radio and local device-to-device links where conventional internet access is unavailable or unnecessary.

What we are measuring

Each experiment looks at what can be carried, how the medium changes the data, and whether the intended recipient can recover it. Results depend on the specific transport and are still being evaluated.
